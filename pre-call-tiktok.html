Call Playbook
🎯
Mode: Diagnose, don't pitch
She wants to feel heard first. She's managing ADHD, solo overwhelm, and a conversion gap that probably makes her feel like she's doing something wrong. She isn't. Tell her that early.
🔓
Open with the reframe
Before she even explains the problem, meet her where she is:
"You've built something real - the brand, the framework, the offer stack. The problem isn't visibility or even your content. The problem is what happens between someone discovering you and paying you. Once we fix that part, the revenue follows."
💰
If she pushes on price
Lead with ROI, not the number. She already has an engaged audience. The content is working. This is plumbing, not building from scratch.
"You already have the audience. We're not starting from zero. If we convert even two or three more clients per month from your existing reach, the investment pays itself back in month one."
🧠
Nervous system match
She works in trauma-informed, somatic space. Match her energy - regulated, warm, unhurried. No high-pressure energy. She'll feel it immediately if you're selling rather than listening.
📌
Find her referral source
She came via word of mouth. Find out who sent her. That connection is gold for how you frame the relationship.
